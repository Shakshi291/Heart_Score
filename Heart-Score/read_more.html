Our Team


Behind Heart Score is a dedicated team of researchers, and technology experts passionate about promoting heart health. Together, we strive to leverage cutting-edge advancements in healthcare technology to deliver the most up-to-date and evidence-based heart health assessments.


What We Offer:


1.Comprehensive Heart Health Assessments: Heart Score offers a range of assessments designed to evaluate various aspects of your heart health. Our assessments take into account factors such as lifestyle, medical history, family history.
2.Personalized Recommendations: Based on the results of your heart health assessment. Our recommendations include lifestyle modifications, exercise routines, dietary guidelines, and potential medical interventions if necessary.
3.Educational Resources: We believe that knowledge is key to maintaining a healthy heart. Heart Score offers a wealth of educational resource. Stay informed about the latest research, prevention strategies, and treatment options to make informed decisions about your cardiovascular well-being.
4.Ongoing Support: At Heart Score, we are committed to supporting you throughout your heart health journey. Connect with others who share similar experiences, ask questions, and find the support you need to make positive changes for your heart health.
