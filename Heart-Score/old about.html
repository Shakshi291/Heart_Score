About Us

"Start from the healthy heart."

Welcome to Heart Score, your trusted source for assessing your heart health and determining whether your heart condition is healthy or not. Our mission is to empower individuals to take control of their cardiovascular well-being and make informed decisions about their lifestyle and healthcare. At Heart Score, we understand the importance of a healthy heart and its impact on overall well- being. We aim to provide accurate, reliable, and easy-to-understand information about heart health, enabling you to monitor and manage your cardiovascular health effectively.
